# Sources

A frozen slice of three public registers, built once by `tools/build_slice.py` so that every
answer the checks compare with stays the same. Registers change every day: this is a snapshot,
not the current state of any company. Nothing here says that a company did anything wrong.

| Folder | Source | Snapshot | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gleif/` | [GLEIF Golden Copy](https://www.gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-golden-copy): Level 2 relationship records (direct and ultimate accounting consolidation), and Level 1 records from the [GLEIF API](https://api.gleif.org) for the entities in the slice | 2026-11-15 | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `companies_house/` | [Companies House](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html): Free Company Data Product, and the [persons with significant control snapshot](https://download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_pscdata.html) | companies 2026-11-01, control 2026-11-15 | [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) |
| `uk_sanctions/` | [UK Sanctions List](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-sanctions-list), Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office | generated 2026-11-12 | [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) |

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## What's in the slice

- **GLEIF:** 34 corporate groups (listed in `tools/groups.txt`), every entity under them through
  direct-parent links, and the parent records GLEIF holds for them.
- **Companies House:** the UK companies GLEIF registers at Companies House (authority RA000585),
  plus the UK companies that own them, up to four levels. Their records of people with
  significant control: corporate owners in full; legal persons (a government body, a building
  society) in full; statements and exemptions.
- **UK Sanctions List:** every designated **entity**. No designated individuals, no ships.

## Personal data

None. An individual with significant control appears only as `kind = individual`, with the
control band and the dates: no name, birth date, nationality or address. Designated individuals
are left out. The only named people in this repository are in Quezal, and they are fictional.

## Quezal

`data/quezal/` is not a source: Quezal is a fictional jurisdiction (code QZ, from the range ISO
3166 leaves for user assignment), generated by `src/trail/quezal/`. Its companies never own, and
are never owned by, a real company, and none shares a name with a company in the slice.
